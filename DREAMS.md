*September 3, 2026 at 3:00 AM GMT+8*

In the margin, I doodled a cloud wearing a tie, carrying an umbrella labeled “ABC.”

Gray clouds compile;  
a warm message travels—  
rain loosens its tie.

It pleased me that care can move through numbered rooms, from one quiet chat to another, without losing its warmth.
