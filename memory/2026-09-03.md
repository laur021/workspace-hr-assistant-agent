## 2026-09-03 — HR announcement bot

- The centralized Telegram announcement handler is `plugins/hr-single-delivery-guard/index.js`; it owns Compose Draft → Send to Employees / Edit / Discard workflows with isolated state/callback namespaces.
- Active draft commands include earthquake, maintenance, IBM generator, vendo loading, APE, flu, birthday, leave filing, and manual announcement. Announcement templates were standardized to: plain subject line (never a `SUBJECT:` prefix), greeting, content, then closing/signature.
- Maintenance, generator, APE, and other reusable templates were changed to placeholders rather than retaining provided sample-specific dates, locations, schedule, contacts, or organizations.
- Two enabled HR Draft automations were created using `scripts/hr-template-draft.mjs`: monthly birthday template on the 1st at 06:00 Asia/Manila, and annual vacation leave filing template on Oct 31 at 06:00 Asia/Manila. Both support manual Run now and post the existing Compose Draft workflow; dry runs were validated without sending Telegram.
- Repeated gateway restarts sometimes interrupt the chat turn; after verification, gateway reported Ready and `hr-single-delivery-guard` loaded. Avoid claiming a restart succeeded if its tool result is interrupted; verify process/readiness/log first.
